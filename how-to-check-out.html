How to Check Out a Book

It's easy! Follow these three simple steps and you'll be reading in no time.

1

Sign in

Tap Sign in up in the corner. Use the class username and password your librarian gave you. It only takes a second!

2

Pick a book

Head to the Catalog and search for something you like. If a book shows available, tap Check out. That's it — it's yours!

3

Read & return

Enjoy your book! Bring it back before its due date. You'll see your due date in My Library.

How long can I keep a book?

It depends on the type of book — and popular books can have a shorter loan so more friends can enjoy them.

TypeUsual loanNotes
Novel / Chapter book14 daysMost books fall here
Graphic novel10 daysQuick, fun reads
Non-fiction21 daysGreat for research
Textbook28 daysYou'll need it all term
Reference5 daysAsk before borrowing
Hot books = shorter loans! If a book is super popular, your loan might be a little shorter so everyone gets a turn. You can always renew once if no one is waiting for it.

Quick questions

What if I don't finish in time?

You can renew once from My Library — unless another student is waiting for it.

What if the book is already checked out?

Tap Place hold to get in line. We'll hold it for you when it comes back!

What if I lose or damage a book?

Accidents happen. Let your librarian know and we'll figure out a fair replacement price together. Every book has a value listed in the catalog.

What if my book is overdue?

No stress — just return it as soon as you can so a friend can read it too. You'll see a friendly reminder in My Library.
